3. What the Application does

  • It reads publicly available headlines and summaries from automotive news RSS feeds.
  • It verifies the figures in those items against primary sources or independent reporting.
  • It writes an original short script, then renders a vertical video made entirely of typography and motion graphics generated by the Application itself, with a synthetic voice-over and a generated audio bed.
  • It uploads the finished video to the Operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

Figures that cannot be confirmed against a primary source or two independent outlets are left out of the video.
