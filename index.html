Auto Décodé

Automotive news,
decoded in 45 seconds.

AUTO DÉCODÉ is a private publishing tool. Once a day it turns a verified automotive news figure — a fuel price record, a registration statistic, a new regulation — into a short vertical video, and posts it to a single TikTok account owned by its operator.

How it works

  1. Reads the public RSS feeds of French automotive news outlets and ranks the day's stories.
  2. Checks every figure against a primary source, or two independent outlets. Anything that cannot be confirmed is left out.
  3. Writes an original script and renders it as typographic motion design — no third-party images, video or audio, ever. Sources are displayed on screen.
  4. Publishes to its operator's own TikTok account through the Content Posting API.
This application is not open to the public. It has no sign-up and no user accounts, and it can only post to the one account that authorised it. Videos are assembled automatically and narrated by a synthetic voice; they contain no realistic scenes and no depictions of real people.
