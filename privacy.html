AUTO DÉCODÉ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Data controller

AUTO DÉCODÉ (“the Application”) is operated by Tony Hurtel, an individual based in France, who acts as data controller within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: tony.hurtel@yahoo.fr.

2. Who this policy covers

The Application is a private publishing tool with a single user: its own operator. It has no sign-up, no user accounts, and no way for a third party to connect to it. It therefore holds personal data relating to one person only — the operator — and never collects data about the viewers, followers, or commenters of the TikTok account it posts to.

4. What the Application does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or process data about TikTok viewers, followers, or commenters.
  • It does not read private messages, contacts, or any account other than the one that authorised it.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share personal data with anyone.
  • It uses no advertising or tracking technology. These policy pages are static and set no cookies.

5. Legal basis

Processing rests on the operator's consent, given explicitly through TikTok's authorisation screen, and on the legitimate interest of operating a tool the operator built for their own account. Consent can be withdrawn at any time (see section 8).

6. Third-party services involved

The Application relies on a small number of external services to do its work:

  • TikTok — receives the video file and its caption in order to publish it, and issues the authorisation tokens described above. TikTok's own Privacy Policy governs what it does with that data.
  • Microsoft Edge text-to-speech — receives the script text of each video in order to return a synthetic voice-over. No personal data is included in that text; it is the narration of a news item.
  • Public RSS feeds of automotive news outlets — read for headlines and summaries. These are ordinary anonymous web requests.

7. Security

Authorisation tokens are stored in a private configuration accessible only to the operator, are never embedded in published videos, and are transmitted only over encrypted connections. TikTok rotates the refresh token periodically and the Application always keeps the most recent one. If a token is believed to be exposed, the operator revokes the application's access from the TikTok app and issues a new one.

8. Your rights, and how to revoke access

Under the GDPR, the operator — as the only person whose data is held — may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, or portability of that data at any time, and may lodge a complaint with the French supervisory authority, the CNIL.

Authorisation can be withdrawn at any moment from the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Once revoked, the Application can no longer publish anything, and the stored tokens become void and are deleted.

9. Children

The Application is not directed at children and is not usable by anyone other than its operator. No data concerning minors is collected.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Application evolves. The current version always lives at https://example.github.io/auto-decode/privacy.html, with the revision date shown at the top of this page.
